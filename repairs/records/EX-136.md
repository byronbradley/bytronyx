## Problem

Xbox 360 displayed a general hardware failure indicator and would not boot.

## Checks

The secondary error code was 0001. A working power supply gave the same fault. I found a drain-to-source short on a MOSFET in the motherboard voltage-regulator circuit.

## Repair

I replaced the shorted MOSFET with a matching part. The short was gone afterward, and the 12 V input rail measured 12.1 V with the console running.

## Parts

Replacement N-channel power MOSFET.

## Testing

I checked startup after the power-circuit repair and ran different games, including cutscenes; the fault did not return.
