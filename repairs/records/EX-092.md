## Problem

Games failed to load or stopped during loading. One USB port did not recognize a known-good controller.

## Checks

Working discs still would not load after cleaning. They loaded when I tried a replacement optical pickup. Same controller worked on another port; damaged port contacts were visible.

## Repair

I replaced the laser pickup with a KES-450A and replaced the damaged USB socket with a matching donor USB Type-A socket. I checked the drive cables and nearby USB connections.

## Parts

KES-450A Blu-ray laser pickup and a donor USB Type-A socket matched to the original footprint.

## Testing

I ran different games to check disc loading and video output, and checked controller detection on the USB ports.
