Disc spun inconsistently and games would not start.

## Checks

I found intermittent disc rotation and loading. A working KSM-440ADM assembly restored consistent operation.

## Repair

I replaced the optical-drive assembly with a KSM-440ADM unit and reconnected its ribbon cable and motor connector.

## Parts

KSM-440ADM optical-drive assembly, including the laser, spindle motor and sled mechanism.

## Testing

I checked disc spin, loading and video with different games.
