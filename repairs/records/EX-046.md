## Problem

Would not read known-good game discs. Controller input failed on one console port.

## Checks

Several clean discs would not load. I found dirt on the lens. Two known-good controllers failed on the same port; connector inspection found damaged contacts.

## Repair

I cleaned the accessible optical lens and reseated the drive connections. Replaced the damaged port assembly and reseated its connection.

## Parts

Optical cleaning supplies; no replacement module. Replacement controller-port assembly.

## Testing

I ran games to check loading and checked controller input through the repaired port.
