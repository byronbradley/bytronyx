## Problem

The A, B, and C buttons did not register consistently.

## Checks

Input checks traced the fault to the A, B, and C button-contact membrane.

## Repair

I cleaned the board contacts and replaced the A, B, and C silicone button-contact membrane with one from a matching donor.

## Parts

Three-button Sega Genesis controller silicone A/B/C contact membrane from a matching donor.

## Testing

I checked the A, B, and C buttons and the remaining controls after replacing the membrane.
