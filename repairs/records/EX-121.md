## Problem

Xbox 360 displayed a general hardware failure indicator and would not boot.

## Checks

I checked the secondary error code and tested the external power supply. I traced the fault to an N-channel MOSFET in the motherboard buck regulator.

## Repair

I replaced the faulty N-channel MOSFET and checked the power rail before reassembling the console.

## Parts

Replacement N-channel power MOSFET in the motherboard buck-regulator stage.

## Testing

I checked startup after the power-circuit repair and ran different games, including cutscenes; the fault did not return.
