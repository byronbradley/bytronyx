# About the repair notes

I put this log together from my spreadsheets, repair notes, recollections, and photos.
I left out measurements and exact part numbers where I did not have them.

Where the register lists a 60-minute test-session limit, that is the maximum
length of a session, not a recorded gameplay duration. Blank limits are unspecified.
For pending repairs, the limit applies to planned testing.

Some photos show several consoles of the same model. I keep those in the general
gallery unless I can match a pictured unit to a specific repair.

White-background photos are for the product gallery. The business page has the
stock-buying, storage, and bench photos.
